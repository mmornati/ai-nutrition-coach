How to grow Back of packet

  1. SowInstallOne command, bin/ai-nutrition-coach up, or the image on ghcr.io. Installs as an app, works offline.
  2. ThinLogPhoto, barcode, words, voice or a pasted recipe. Saved meals and recents for the rest.
  3. HarvestReviewThe Day Board shows what is there and what is not. The coach asks one question.
