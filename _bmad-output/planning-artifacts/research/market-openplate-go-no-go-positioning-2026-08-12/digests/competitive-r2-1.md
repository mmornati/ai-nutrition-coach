## Resolved conflicts

### Cal AI ARR: $30M vs $40M (and later $50M)

The number TechCrunch used in its original acquisition article is **USD **30M+** (annual revenue**, "in under two years"), not $40M. Dealroom's reaction headline is the origin of "$40M in annual sales"; GetLatka and Sparrowapps repeat ~$40M ARR; ARR Club and Superframeworks later log $50M. So:
- **$30M+** — TechCrunch (2026-03-02), the primary source the brief asked to settle. Confidence: High.
- **$40M** — Dealroom headline + GetLatka ($35M→$40M) + Sparrowapps. Confidence: Medium; secondhand/tracker-derived.
- **$50M** — ARR Club milestone + Superframeworks, both post-acquisition self-reports. Confidence: Low-Medium.

State plainly: at deal announcement the defensible figure is **$30M+ ARR** (TechCrunch); the $40M and $50M are downstream tracker/Fund-raising summaries of the same story as revenue kept climbing, not corrections of TechCrunch.
