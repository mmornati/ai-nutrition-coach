## COPPA landscape — the compliance design points

1. **Status/timeline**: final amendments published 2025-04-22, effective 2025-06-23, full compliance required by 2026-04-22 (already passed). Penalties up to $53,088/violation, computable per child per day. Enforcement posture remains active (PlayOn Sports 2026 CalPrivacy fine; multiple 2025 FTC actions).
2. **Where photos sit:** "personal information" now includes biometric identifiers — facial templates/faceprints/voiceprints. Plain plate photos WITHOUT face/voice recognition are not per se biometric, but any "who ate this"/face- or voice-enabled family feature touching under-13 data triggers the biometric consent apparatus. Facial/photo identity signals on kids = COPPA-covered; meal photo logging on a parent's account is the safe lane. Also: the FTC declined "data derived from biometrics," so inference outputs alone don't trigger the definition — an argument for local-inference design.
3. **Age-gates:** the Kurbo precedent established that a non-neutral age screen ("parent signup, or at least 13") encouraged kids to lie and was treated as a violation; the amended rule now requires *neutral* screening that doesn't default to an age or encourage falsification. Design rule: no "adult implies 13+" shortcuts; get age via neutral flow; treat any under-13 signal as COPPA-triggered.
4. **2026 age-verification signal:** FTC Policy Statement (2026-02-25) allows collection of children's data SOLELY for age verification without parental consent on mixed/general-audience services — if use is limited to age verification, data is secured, deleted once verification ends, and privacy notices say so. This is the sanctioned path for "verify, don't profile."
5. **Consent architecture:** separate verifiable parental consent required for any third-party disclosure (advertising, AI training); "support for internal operations" is the only carve-out — so an optional-AI product must decide whether AI runs locally (internal) or via third-party API (disclosure → separate consent). Text-message consent is now an approved method.
6. **Data minimization/retention:** written retention policy under a "reasonably necessary" standard; Kurbo's order set a 1-year-after-last-use ceiling precedent — a concrete, conservative default for a family nutrition product.
7. **Structural option:** adults-only (18+/13+) self-hosted core with no under-13 account creation sidesteps most of this; any "family/kids profiles" feature re-enters the full matrix. Self-hosting ≠ immunization — a mixed-audience app is COPPA-exposed by direction/audience, not by where the server runs.
