The Calm Coach

Spa restraint taken to its logical end: no cards, no borders, no chrome — just large quiet type and one muted green, reserved for progress and the single action worth taking. The empty plate reads as a held breath, and the coach whispers in italic serif instead of announcing. This is the direction that most strongly refuses to shout.

Specs for this direction: display weights 200–400, leading generous · cards replaced by hairline rules and whitespace · exactly one color in play (sage #6E8376) · the only elevated element is the single sage + button and the sage "Sure" pill · microcopy leans on rest, not reward · the week strip uses dots, never bars or counts.
