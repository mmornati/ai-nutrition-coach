Fresh Food-Forward

The meal is the hero: photograph-sized color plates, bright-but-not-pastel washes of tomato, basil, mustard and butter, and playful rounded type that stays adult. Capturing by snapshot feels like the natural instinct here — the camera tile is the loudest thing on screen, because food is what this app is really about.

Specs for this direction: photo-sized gradient plates replacing text-first cards · bold bite-pills for groups (on/off state per color) · favorite colors: berry #E4573D, basil #4A9B68, sun #F5B423 · rounded-bundle type · the dent is drawn as a leafy dashed ring · every state keeps the taste-line, so logging feels like remembering, not reporting.
