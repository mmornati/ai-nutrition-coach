Dev-Friendly · Self-Host

An app that quietly wears its engineering on its sleeve: bordered ledger-style cards, mono status chips, and a status line that tells you your instance is up. Still warm — the baked-orange accent and moss green keep it a kitchen tool, not a terminal. The about view is the full "yours to run" flourish, worn proudly.

Specs for this direction: 8–10px radii, 1.5px borders, hairline dividers · mono labels for everything machine-ish (state, qty, version, storage) · sans for readable warmth · baked-orange #E8833A reserved for action + snapshot · moss #375A3D and sprig #4F7A54 for calm balance · status chips build "self-host pride" without sacrificing warmth.
