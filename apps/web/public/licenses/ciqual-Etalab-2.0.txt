Anses. 2025. Ciqual French food composition table, version 2025.
DOI 10.57745/RDMHWY (data revision of 2025-11-03, published 2025-11-19).

The per-100 g nutrient values of the built-in food catalogue (energy, protein, carbohydrate, sugars,
fat, saturated fat, fibre, salt) and the food names and groups come from the Ciqual table, published
by the Observatoire des aliments of the French Agency for Food, Environmental and Occupational
Health and Safety (Anses), and are used under the Etalab Open Licence version 2.0
("Licence Ouverte / Open Licence", compatible with CC BY 4.0).

Changes made by AI Nutrition Coach: the information was extracted from the official XML files
(alim, alim_grp, compo, const), reduced to eight nutrients per food, and converted to a compact
JSON file (tools/ciqual/build-ciqual.mjs). Values published as "traces" are stored as 0; values
published as "< x" are stored as 0 when x is at most 0.1 and as unknown otherwise; values
published as "-" are unknown. Numbers are rounded to two decimals. Ciqual values are averages
for French foods; they are not the values of a given product. Anses has not reviewed or endorsed
this application.
